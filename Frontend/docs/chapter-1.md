## Core Concepts

AI-driven book creation represents a revolutionary approach to content generation and publishing. This chapter introduces the fundamental concepts and tools that make up our AI-powered book creation pipeline.

### What is AI-driven authoring?

AI-driven authoring refers to the process of using artificial intelligence tools and systems to assist in or automate the creation of written content. This includes generating text, structuring content, formatting documents, and even creating entire chapters based on specifications and prompts.

### The Tools in Our Stack

Our AI-driven book creation pipeline uses several key tools:

- **Spec-Kit Plus**: For structured AI-driven development
- **Claude Code**: For professional AI writing and coding
- **Docusaurus**: For modern documentation-style book publishing
- **GitHub Pages**: For live public hosting
